3. Drift: measured against the noise model

Each segment aligns on 10 s (bias and gravity from the mean), then integrates freely for 20 s: gyro → attitude error θ, accelerometer rotated through θ (a + θ × a, which is where tilt leaks gravity into position) → velocity → position. Measured = RMS over 2 undisturbed segments. Predicted 1σ: heading √((N√t)² + (b t)²) and position √2 · √((N_a t^1.5/√3)² + (b_a t²/2)² + (g b_g t³/6)²), where each bias b = √(B² + N²/t_align) includes the error of the 10 s alignment average.
